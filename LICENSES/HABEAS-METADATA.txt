Courts, and the Singapore Courts) and are NOT redistributed by this
project. data/raw/ is gitignored and must not be committed.

Why a custom licence rather than CC-BY-4.0
------------------------------------------

The DIFC, ADGM, and Singapore Courts terms of use prohibit reproduction
and redistribution of website content. CC-BY-4.0 expressly permits
commercial redistribution by downstream users; that grant is incompatible
with the source ToS. A non-commercial-research, takedown-respecting
licence is the closest open-licence shape that does not exceed our
underlying rights. See data/tos_audit.md for the full audit.

     difccourts.ae, adgm.com, assets.adgm.com, or elitigation.sg.
