If hand-validation against named cases yields a PR3 < 2 for ICCP-CA Paris, the rubric requires explicit civil-law adaptation. If hand-validation yields a per-ruling overall < 1.85 for any peer, the rubric over-credits the DIFC/ADGM/SICC sample.
